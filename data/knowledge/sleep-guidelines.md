# Sleep Guidelines

Adults typically need 7–9 hours of sleep per night for optimal health. Consistent sleep schedules help regulate circadian rhythms.

## Tips for better sleep
- Keep a regular bedtime and wake time, even on weekends.
- Limit screen exposure 30–60 minutes before bed.
- Keep the bedroom cool, dark, and quiet.
- Avoid heavy meals and caffeine late in the evening.

## When to seek help
Persistent insomnia, loud snoring, or daytime fatigue may warrant consultation with a healthcare provider.

Structured sleep improvement programs can teach relaxation techniques, sleep hygiene, and cognitive strategies for better rest.
