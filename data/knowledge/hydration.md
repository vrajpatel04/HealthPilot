# Hydration

Water supports digestion, temperature regulation, joint health, and cognitive function.

## Daily intake
Most adults benefit from roughly 2–3 liters of fluids daily, adjusted for activity level, climate, and individual needs.

## Signs of adequate hydration
- Pale yellow urine
- Regular thirst without excessive dryness
- Stable energy during daily activities

Hydration habits pair well with nutrition and fitness goals in holistic wellness programs.
