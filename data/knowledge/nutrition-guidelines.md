# Nutrition Guidelines

A balanced diet supports energy, immunity, and long-term wellness. Focus on whole foods: vegetables, fruits, lean proteins, whole grains, and healthy fats.

## Core principles
- Eat a variety of colorful produce daily.
- Choose minimally processed foods when possible.
- Stay hydrated throughout the day.
- Practice portion awareness rather than strict restriction.

## Meal planning
Planning meals ahead reduces reliance on convenience foods and supports consistent nutrition goals.

Nutrition coaching programs can help personalize meal plans and build sustainable eating habits.
