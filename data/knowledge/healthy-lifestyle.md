# Healthy Lifestyle

Wellness is the combination of sleep, nutrition, movement, mental health, and healthy habits sustained over time.

## Pillars
1. **Sleep** — consistent rest and recovery
2. **Nutrition** — balanced, whole-food eating
3. **Activity** — regular movement you enjoy
4. **Mind** — stress management and emotional balance
5. **Connection** — community and purpose

Marketplace programs that integrate multiple pillars often produce the most lasting behavior change.
