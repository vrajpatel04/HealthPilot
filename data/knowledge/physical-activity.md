# Physical Activity

Regular movement reduces risk of chronic disease, improves mood, and supports healthy sleep.

## Recommendations
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week.
- Include muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days.
- Break up long sitting periods with short walks or stretches.

## Getting started
Begin with activities you enjoy — walking, cycling, swimming, or structured fitness programs — to build consistency.

Fitness programs with progressive plans help beginners and intermediate exercisers stay accountable.
