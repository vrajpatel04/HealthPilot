    # Stress Management

Chronic stress affects sleep, mood, and physical health. Proactive stress management improves overall wellbeing.

## Techniques
- Deep breathing and mindfulness meditation
- Regular physical activity
- Social connection and supportive relationships
- Time boundaries for work and rest

## Programs
Structured mental wellness programs teach cognitive reframing, relaxation exercises, and resilience skills for everyday stressors.

Seek professional support if stress feels overwhelming or persistent.
